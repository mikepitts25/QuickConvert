# Travel Quick Convert Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026

Travel Quick Convert is a unit conversion utility from Colibri Code LLC.

## Information We Collect

Travel Quick Convert does not require an account and does not ask you to enter personal information.

The app may display advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and other information as described in Google's advertising and privacy policies. Colibri Code LLC does not receive information that directly identifies you from the app.

## How Information Is Used

The app uses conversion input only on your device to calculate unit conversions. Advertising data may be used by Google AdMob to deliver, measure, and improve ads.

## Data Sharing

Colibri Code LLC does not sell personal information. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google AdMob according to Google's policies.

## Children

Travel Quick Convert is not directed to children under 13.
